What you do

A tower game about the ad business

Every floor is a department. Every creative has a dream role. Every client wants it yesterday.

🏗️

Stack floors

32 floor types across Creative, Strategy, Media, Production and Account. Each makes three tiers of work, from a Tagline to a Full Ad Suite, and it sells while you're away.

🧑‍🎨

Hire creatives

Every creative arrives with skills, a quirk and a dream role. Put them on the floor they always wanted and they work harder. Level them to 20, unlock perks, kit them out with gear.

🛗

Drive the lift

Tap a visitor, then press and hold to ride them up. Land on their floor for a tip, land it neatly for a bonus. The rare Headhunter, Award Judge or Big Spender pays a lot more.

💼

Win the brief

Sixty fictional brands walk in with briefs. Choose between up to three clients, reserve the stock, check your odds, then deliver and sign them as a retained account.

📋

Keep or resign

Cash cows pay the rent and never win anything. Trophy accounts lose money and win awards. Startups double or vanish. The roster has a cap, so you decide who stays.

✨

Grow it taller

Floor upgrades, a 12-perk Reputation tree, 30 pieces of gear, 36 achievements and daily missions. At 50 floors, go public: start again with permanent share bonuses and a senior creative in tow.

Between floors

Always something to chase

The tower builds itself while you're gone. These are the reasons to come back.

🏆 The New Business League

From 10 floors, your agency joins a table of seven for a three-day season. Win briefs, claim missions and open floors to score; beat a rival in a pitch and they lose points too. Climb from Local to Regional, National and Global.

📅 A new week, a new event

Every Monday brings a themed week: Talent Week, Pitch Season, Open House, Training Week or Awards Season. Each tilts the odds your way, and the hub shows what's coming next.

🏃 Two daily mini-games

Rush Hour is a 60-second lift frenzy. In Deadline Dash, your top creative runs the work across town, jumping benches, cones and hydrants as the street speeds up. Both keep your personal best.

Screens

Every floor is a tiny scene

A city skyline drifts behind the tower, rooms fill with furniture as you upgrade them, and the staff wander about minding their own business.

How it plays

The loop

Borrowed from the best tower games, re-themed for anyone who has sat through a status call.

Build a floor and pick a department.
Creatives move into the studios. Hire the ones who dream of that floor.
Start a job: a Tagline, a Print Ad, a Client Dinner. The work sells on its own.
Tap the visitor in the lobby and hold to ride them to their floor for a tip.
A client walks in with a brief. Deliver from stock and sign them as an account.
Accounts bill by the hour, and every brief scores league points. Keep the cash cows, gamble on a trophy.
Spend Reputation on perks, upgrade floors, go public, and do it again, taller.
🎵

An original 8-bit soundtrack

Six chiptune tracks made for the game: four for the tower, two for the mini-games. Every event has its own sound, and music steps aside when you're playing your own.

☁️

Plays nice

Offline earnings and iCloud sync, so the agency follows you between devices. Sessions are 30 seconds or 30 minutes; the agency keeps billing either way.

♿

Built for everyone

Larger text, an assisted lift, a VoiceOver floors menu and reduced motion. Sound, music and haptics each have their own switch.
