What you do

A tower game about the ad business

Every floor is a department. Every worker has a dream job. Every client wants it yesterday.

🏗️

Stack floors

Creative, Strategy, Media, Production and Account departments — each producing three tiers of work that sells while you're away.

🧑‍🎨

Hire creatives

Procedurally generated staff with skills, quirks and a dream role. Put them where they belong for double output — and level them up to 20.

🛗

Run the lift

Tap a visitor, drag the car up the open shaft, drop them on the right floor. Clients tip; the rare Headhunter, Award Judge or Big Spender does a lot more.

💼

Win campaigns

Clients arrive with briefs. Deliver the work from your own stock, beat rival agencies on quality, hang their logo on the wall.

✨

Upgrade everything

Floor levels, a four-branch perk tree, gear for your creatives, and a Holding Company prestige when you reach 50 floors.

☁️

Plays nice

Offline earnings, daily missions, achievements, iCloud sync. Sessions are 30 seconds or 30 minutes — your call.

Screens

Every floor is a tiny scene

Pixel art from the Pixel Spaces pack, a city skyline that scrolls behind the tower, hand-placed furniture, and people who wander around and mind their own business.

How it plays

The loop

Borrowed from the best tower games, re-themed for people who've sat through a status call.

Build a floor and pick a department.
Creatives move into the studios. Hire the ones who dream of that floor.
Produce a Tagline, a Print Ad, a Super Bowl Ad. They sell on their own.
Tap the visitor in the lobby and drag the lift to their floor for a tip.
A client walks in with a brief. Deliver from stock, win the account.
Spend Reputation on perks, upgrade floors, go public, do it again — taller.
