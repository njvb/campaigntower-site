1FThe game

Build it, staff it, bill it.

Borrowed from the best tower games, re-themed for anyone who has sat through a status call.

Build

Every floor is a department: Copywriting, Social, the Photo Studio, a Rooftop Bar. Each makes three tiers of work, from a Tagline to a Full Ad Suite, and sells it while you're away.

Hire

Three creatives to a floor. Put them in the role they've always dreamed of and they work harder. Level them to 20 and kit them out with gear.

Win

A client walks in with a brief. Deliver it from stock and they sign as an account, paying you by the hour. Then you decide who stays.

4FBetween floors

Always something to chase.

The tower builds itself while you're gone. These are the reasons to come back.

The New Business League

At 10 floors you join a table of seven agencies for a three-day season. Win briefs, claim missions and open floors to score. Beat a rival in a pitch and they lose points too. Climb from Local to Regional, National and Global.

A new event every Monday

Each week tilts the odds your way, and the hub tells you what's coming next.

Talent WeekPitch SeasonOpen HouseTraining WeekAwards Season

Rush Hour

Once a day: sixty seconds, one lift, everybody at once. Your best round goes on the board.

Deadline Dash

Your best creative grabs the work and runs it across town. Benches, cones, hydrants, a suspiciously placed bucket. The street speeds up the further you get, and your personal best is always watching.

6FHouse rules

Fair, free and friendly.

Sessions are 30 seconds or 30 minutes. The agency keeps billing either way.

Free to play

Optional rewarded ads and three purchases: two Awards packs and the Agency Pass. No energy meter, no paywalled floors.

Plays while you don't

Offline earnings while you're away, and iCloud sync so the agency follows you between devices.

Built for everyone

Larger text, an assisted lift, a VoiceOver floors menu and reduced motion. Sound, music and haptics each have their own switch.
